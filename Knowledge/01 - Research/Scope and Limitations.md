# Scope and Limitations

## Scope (proposal)
- Design and technical development of the BantayAralan system: real-time behavior detection and clutter identification.
- Automated alerts and event-based image capture as visual evidence for behavioral review.
- Intended for implementation within **public grade school classrooms**, focused on technology–educator interaction.
- Scheduled for implementation and evaluation in early 2026 (per the proposal's original timeline — not confirmed as still current).

## Limitations and exclusions (proposal)
- Not designed to replace teacher authority or decision-making — a support tool only.
- Excludes long-term psychological profiling of students; focuses on immediate, event-based disruptive actions.
- Technical constraint acknowledged: potentially reduced detection accuracy in highly cluttered environments or with significant object overlap.
- Constrained by ethical requirements: captured data must support review without identifying individual students.

## Status
**Proposal Requirement.** No implementation has been evaluated against these limitations yet — there is no detection pipeline to test. See [[04 - Computer Vision]], [[10 - Privacy & Ethics]].
