# Research Questions & Objectives

From the proposal's working draft. Each objective roughly maps to the research question addressing the same capability.

## Research questions
1. How can a computer vision-based behavior detection system be designed and developed to identify disruptive student behaviors in classrooms?
2. How accurate is the system in detecting disruptive behaviors and scattered objects in real time?
3. How effective is the system in capturing event-based images that support teachers in reviewing detected behaviors?
4. How timely are the alerts generated by the system in assisting teacher intervention during classroom activities?
5. How can privacy and ethical considerations be ensured in the capture and storage of images related to disruptive events without identifying individual students?

## Objectives
1. **Real-time detection model** — create a system using computer vision to accurately identify disruptive behaviors as they happen.
2. **Automated cleanliness monitor** — detect scattered objects/clutter to help maintain an organized learning environment.
3. **Event-based evidence capture** — capture visual evidence of specific disruptive events to assist teachers in reviewing student behavior.
4. **Timely alert system** — provide rapid notifications supporting immediate teacher intervention.
5. **Ethical data protocols** — capture and store images in a way that prioritizes student privacy without identifying individuals.

## Status
**Proposal Requirement.** None of these objectives has a working implementation yet; the two admin UI prototypes ([[UI UX Overview]]) address only the "review evidence" side (objective 3) with mock data, not live detection.
